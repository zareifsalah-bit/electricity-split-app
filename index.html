Calculation Logic

Fully automatic

👥 Both available: each pays 50% of that day's cost.
✈️ Only one person on vacation: the other person pays 100% for those days.
🌍 Both on vacation: those days remain 50/50.
⚙️ Automatic: changing vacations immediately recalculates every affected bill and all totals.
💳 Payment allocation: payments are applied automatically to the oldest outstanding bills first (FIFO), while each person's payments settle that person's oldest share first.
